About us

Sone Restaurant on Dumraon Station Road is a family restaurant serving veg and non-veg tandoor, chicken, paneer, dal, rice, egg and Chinese dishes. Enjoy dine-in, pickup, or home delivery within 5 km in Dumraon.

Our Party Hall is available for birthdays, anniversaries, kitty parties and family functions.
